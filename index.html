Why Targum

Everything you need to translate. Nothing you don't.

⌨️

Global Hotkey

Select text in any app, press Ctrl+Shift+T, and get a translation instantly — no copy-paste, no tab-switching. Fully configurable.

🔄

Multiple Engines

Switch between translation engines on the fly. Compare results and trust the one that fits best.

🔊

Text-to-Speech

Listen to your translation with live word-by-word highlighting. Supports US and British English accents.

🌍

20 Languages

Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Russian, and 14 more. Full RTL rendering for Hebrew, Arabic, and Persian.

🗂️

System Tray

Targum lives quietly in your system tray. It never gets in the way — until you need it.

⚡

Lightweight

Built with Tauri — a Rust-powered backend paired with a Vue frontend. Fast startup, minimal memory footprint.
